Learn It

When you see a new word, look for prefixes and suffixes you already know. Add their meaning to the base word, and you can often figure out what the whole word means.

  • 'unhelpful' = un (not) + help + ful (full of) = not full of help
  • 'preview' = pre (before) + view = to see before
  • 'kindness' = kind + ness (state of being) = being kind
  • 'reusable' = re (again) + use + able (can be) = can be used again
