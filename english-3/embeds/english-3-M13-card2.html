Learn It

The main idea is what a passage is mostly about. Supporting details are the smaller facts that tell more about the main idea. Think of the main idea as the trunk of a tree and the details as the branches.

  • Main idea: 'Bees help plants grow.'
  • Detail: Bees carry pollen from flower to flower.
  • Detail: Without bees, many fruits would not grow.
  • Detail: Bees also make honey for people to enjoy.
