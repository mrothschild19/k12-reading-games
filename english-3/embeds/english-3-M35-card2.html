Learn It

Great writing happens in steps. First you PLAN your ideas. Then you DRAFT (write a first try). Next you REVISE (make ideas better). Then you EDIT (fix spelling and punctuation). Finally, you PUBLISH and share.

  • Plan: list ideas or draw a web
  • Draft: write the first version - don't worry about being perfect
  • Revise: add details, take out parts that do not fit
  • Edit: fix spelling, capital letters, and punctuation
