A glossary is like a mini dictionary at the back of a book. A dictionary is a bigger book full of word meanings. Both help you when context clues are not enough. Words are listed in ABC order.

  • Glossary: at the back of a science book - lists special words
  • Dictionary: gives the meaning, how to say it, and how it is used
  • ABC order: 'apple' comes before 'banana'
  • Guide words: tell the first and last word on each page
