Learn It

An inference is a smart guess based on clues in the story plus what you already know. The author does not say everything. Good readers read between the lines.

  • Clue: She grabbed an umbrella. Inference: It is raining.
  • Clue: He yawned and rubbed his eyes. Inference: He is tired.
  • Clue: Crumbs and an empty plate. Inference: Someone ate a snack.
  • Clue: A wagging tail. Inference: The dog is happy.
