Learn It

When you meet a new word, don't panic. Look at the words and sentences around it - those are context clues. They give you hints about what the word probably means.

  • 'The puppy was timid, or shy, near new people.' - 'shy' explains 'timid'
  • 'It was scorching - so hot the sidewalk burned my feet.' - 'so hot' explains 'scorching'
  • 'She was famished, meaning very hungry.' - the definition is right there
  • 'He chuckled and laughed at the joke.' - 'laughed' gives the clue
