Learn It

Every long word has a strong middle part called the root or base word. The root holds the main meaning. When you find the root, you can often guess what the whole word means.

  • 'replay' - the root is 'play'
  • 'unkindness' - the base is 'kind'
  • 'porter' comes from the root 'port' (to carry)
  • 'telescope' has 'tele' (far) and 'scope' (to look)
