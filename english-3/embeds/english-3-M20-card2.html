Learn It

Sometimes two books tell about the same topic in different ways. Comparing them helps you learn more. Look for what is the same and what is different in the facts and the way they are told.

  • Both texts say bees make honey - same
  • One has a diagram, the other has a story - different
  • Both list flowers bees like - same
  • One is for kids, one is for grown-ups - different
