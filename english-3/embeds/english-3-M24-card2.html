Some words mean almost the same thing, but with different strengths. 'Glad,' 'happy,' and 'thrilled' all mean joyful - but thrilled is the biggest! These tiny differences are called shades of meaning.

  • glad -> happy -> thrilled (more and more joy)
  • warm -> hot -> scorching (hotter and hotter)
  • tired -> sleepy -> exhausted (more and more worn out)
  • scared -> afraid -> terrified (more and more frightened)
