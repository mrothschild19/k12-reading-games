Multisyllabic Decoding: Open & Closed Syllables

Module 03 · Lesson 1
Today's skill: Introducing open & closed syllables
📖 "Let everything that has breath praise the Lord." — Psalm 150:6
💡 Remember:
  • Open syllables: he, go, hi, me, table (ta-ble)
  • Closed syllables: cat, sit, run, napkin (nap-kin)
  • Mixed: ro-bot (open + closed)
