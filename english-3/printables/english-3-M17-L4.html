Comprehension - Character Motivation & Traits

Module 17 · Lesson 4
Today's skill: Practicing character motivation & traits with a passage
📖 "Whatever you do, do it all for the glory of God." — 1 Corinthians 10:31
💡 Remember:
  • Traits: brave, honest, curious, stubborn
  • Motivation: He shared his lunch because he is kind.
  • Ask: What is the character like? Why did they do that?
