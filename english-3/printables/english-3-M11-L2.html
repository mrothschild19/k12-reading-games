Fluency - Rate, Accuracy, Expression

Module 11 · Lesson 2
Today's skill: Reading at a smooth rate
📖 "Apply your heart to instruction." — Proverbs 23:12
💡 Remember:
  • Rate: not too fast, not too slow
  • Accuracy: reading each word correctly
  • Expression: making your voice show excitement, questions, or feelings
