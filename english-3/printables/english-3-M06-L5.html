Today's skill: Using the parts to unlock meaning

💡 Remember:
  • un- = not: unhappy, unlock, unfair
  • re- = again: redo, return, replay
  • pre- = before: preview, preheat
Part 1 — Circle the Correct Answer
  1. What does 'un-' mean in 'unhappy'?

  3. What does 'pre-' mean in 'preview'?
    ( a ) after   ( b ) before   ( c ) not
  4. What does 'mis-' mean in 'misspell'?
