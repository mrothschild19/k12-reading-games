Module 25 · Lesson 2
Today's skill: Spotting abstract nouns in sentences

💡 Remember:
  • Abstract: love, joy, courage, friendship, honesty
  • Concrete (can touch): apple, book, dog
  • Abstract nouns name ideas and feelings
