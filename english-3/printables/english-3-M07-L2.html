Common Suffixes: -ful, -less, -ness, -ment

Module 07 · Lesson 2
Today's skill: What each part means
📖 "The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge." — Proverbs 1:7
💡 Remember:
  • -ful = full of: hopeful, joyful, careful
  • -less = without: hopeless, fearless
  • -ness = state of: kindness, darkness
