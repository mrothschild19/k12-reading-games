Grammar - Regular & Irregular Verbs

Module 26 · Lesson 2
Today's skill: Spotting regular & irregular verbs in sentences
📖 "Your word is a lamp to my feet." — Psalm 119:105
💡 Remember:
  • Regular: walk → walked, play → played
  • Irregular: go → went, eat → ate, see → saw
  • Irregular: run → ran, swim → swam
